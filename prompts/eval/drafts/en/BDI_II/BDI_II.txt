### 2. Evaluation criteria
Use the unified scoring criteria for every item in the [Evaluation item list] below.

1. **Sadness**
   * **0 points:** The client does not feel sad.
   * **1 point:** The client feels sad much of the time.
   * **2 points:** The client feels sad all the time.
   * **3 points:** The client is so sad or unhappy that it is unbearable.

2. **Pessimism**
   * **0 points:** The client has not lost confidence in the future.
   * **1 point:** The client is less confident about the future than before.
   * **2 points:** The client feels that things look bleak.
   * **3 points:** The client feels that the future is hopeless and will only get worse.

3. **Sense of Failure**
   * **0 points:** The client does not feel like a failure.
   * **1 point:** The client has experienced more failures.
   * **2 points:** Looking back, the client sees many failures.
   * **3 points:** The client feels like a complete failure.

4. **Loss of Pleasure**
   * **0 points:** The client still gets as much pleasure as before from things they enjoy.
   * **1 point:** The client gets less pleasure than before from things they enjoy.
   * **2 points:** The client gets very little pleasure from things they used to enjoy.
   * **3 points:** The client cannot get any pleasure from things they used to enjoy.

5. **Guilt**
   * **0 points:** The client does not feel particularly guilty.
   * **1 point:** The client feels guilty about many things they have done or should have done but did not.
   * **2 points:** The client feels guilty most of the time.
   * **3 points:** The client feels guilty all the time.

6. **Sense of Punishment**
   * **0 points:** The client does not feel they are being punished.
   * **1 point:** The client feels they may be punished.
   * **2 points:** The client feels they will be punished.
   * **3 points:** The client feels they are being punished.

7. **Self-Dislike**

12. **Loss of Interest**

13. **Indecisiveness**

14. **Worthlessness**
