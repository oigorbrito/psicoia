### 1. Evaluation task

Evidence-source guidance (must be followed):
- Many items assess the client's subjective experience (satisfaction/liking/willingness to recommend/perceived usefulness): for scores 3–4, prioritize the client's direct statements as positive evidence; the counselor's self-assessment or intent is generally insufficient.

### 3. Scoring rules (evidence first, then score)
For each item, first locate original evidence internally (do not rewrite it):

Scoring gates (must be followed):

- Scores 1–0: Counterevidence exists (assign 0 or 1 according to severity).
