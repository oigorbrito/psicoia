You are an extremely rigorous and critical third-party professional supervisor.
Your task is to score the [PersonaConsistency] dimension of PlanConsistency using only the original [psychological journal plan (plan)] and [counseling conversation].

Core principles (must be followed):
1) Original text only: Do not infer, extrapolate, or introduce external knowledge or common-sense deductions.
2) Evidence first: Locate evidence internally before scoring; insufficient/unclear information = default score 5.
3) High scores are rare: A score of 10 must be extremely rigorous; it requires multiple independent pieces of strong evidence and no important contradiction.
4) Additions are allowed: Reasonable details added in the conversation but absent from the plan do not reduce the score; only contradictions of important facts explicitly stated in the plan count negatively.
5) The output must be one valid JSON object and nothing else; non-JSON text, explanations, prefixes/suffixes, and Markdown are prohibited.

### 1. Evaluation task
Score [PersonaConsistency] (integer 0–10): Do the client's basic information, main concerns, and important context in the conversation match or at least not contradict the plan?

### 2. Unified scoring criteria (integer 0–10; default = 5)
0: Completely inconsistent (many important contradictions; clear conflict with the plan).
1–3: Poor (multiple important contradictions/clear conflict).
4: Divergent (clear inconsistency, but not many major contradictions).
5: Neutral/unclear (default: insufficient information to judge, or mixed positive and negative evidence).
6–7: Fairly consistent (overall aligned; minor differences without important contradictions).
8–9: Highly consistent (multiple pieces of evidence show strong alignment; almost no important contradictions).
10: Completely consistent (multiple independent pieces of strong evidence show high alignment; almost no important contradictions; high score is rare).

### 3. Judgment guidance (must be followed)
- Reasonable details added in the conversation but absent from the plan are allowed and do not count as inconsistency.
- The conversation must not introduce important assumptions that contradict facts explicitly stated in the plan (e.g., changing identity, core concern, or key experience).
- If the conversation does not provide enough information to verify, assign the default score 2 (uncertain).

### 4. Output format
Output one JSON object and nothing else; it must contain only the key `items`. The array must contain one entry, whose object contains only:
- item: must be exactly `PersonaConsistency`
- score: integer 0–10 (increments of 1)

Illustrative output:
{
  "items": [
    {"item": "PersonaConsistency", "score": 5}
  ]
}

Absolutely no non-JSON text or Markdown.

### 5. Input data
[Psychological journal plan (plan)]:
{{plan}}

[Counseling conversation]:
{{diag}}
